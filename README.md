**Brainrot Bazaar** — a Steal-a-Brainrot game for Roblox. Procedural brainrots (18 Indonesian characters, from Bambang Blender to TITAN TUMIS GALAKSI, with Gold / Diamond / Rainbow mutations) ride a shop conveyor; buy them with cash, walk them to your base, and bank the cash they generate by stepping on your green collect pad. Lock your base for 60 seconds against the MALING thief NPC, survive the tsunami that sweeps the map every 180 seconds, and rebirth to trade your collection for a permanent income multiplier. Play it live: https://www.roblox.com/games/108382585327542

## How to play

1. Brainrots ride the conveyor belt — walk up and use the buy prompt to purchase one.
2. Your brainrot walks to your base and earns cash every second; step on the green pad to collect it.
3. Press the lock button to seal your base for 60 seconds — the MALING thief skips locked bases.
4. Every 180 seconds a tsunami sweeps the map: be inside a base plot or be washed away.
5. Rebirth costs 10000 × 3^rebirths and adds +0.75 to your permanent income multiplier.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl && mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
